# AegisLife Insurance Analytics

## Project Overview

AegisLife Insurance Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project developed to analyze insurance customers, policies, claims, fraud risk, agent performance, and customer risk behavior.

The project demonstrates a complete analytics workflow using Python, MySQL, and Power BI, covering data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), hypothesis testing, business insights generation, and dashboard development.

---

## Business Objectives

* Analyze insurance policy performance
* Evaluate claim trends and claim approval patterns
* Identify fraud-related risks
* Assess agent productivity
* Understand customer risk profiles
* Generate actionable business recommendations

## Key Insights

* Vehicle policies are the most sold product category.
* Health insurance generates the highest total claim amount.
* Nearly 49% of claims are fraud-flagged.
* Overall loss ratio is 4.71.
* Policy lapse rate is approximately 32%.
* Around 70% of customers belong to the Medium Risk segment.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Strengthen fraud detection mechanisms.
* Review pricing strategy for high-loss products.
* Improve customer retention programs.
* Monitor high-risk customer segments.
* Track agent-level fraud and lapse metrics.

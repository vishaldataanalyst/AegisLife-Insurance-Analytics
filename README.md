# AegisLife Insurance Analytics

**End-to-End Insurance Analytics Project using Python, SQL, and Power BI including Data Cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Hypothesis Testing, Business Insights, and Interactive Dashboards.**

---

## Project Overview

AegisLife Insurance Analytics is an end-to-end analytics project focused on analyzing insurance operations, customer risk profiles, claims performance, fraud indicators, and agent productivity.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow:

* Data Collection & Validation
* Data Cleaning using Python
* SQL-based Business Analysis
* Statistical Hypothesis Testing
* Power BI Dashboard Development
* Business Insights & Recommendations

## SQL Business Insights

### Claims & Profitability

* Overall loss ratio is 4.71.
* Property insurance has the highest loss ratio.
* Health insurance generated the highest total claim amount.

### Fraud Analysis

* Approximately 49% of claims are fraud-flagged.
* Fraud detection and claim verification should be business priorities.

### Customer Risk

* Around 69% of customers fall into the Medium Risk segment.
* High-risk customers represent roughly 15% of the customer base.

### Policy Retention

* Policy lapse rate is 32%.
* Customer retention initiatives could improve recurring premium revenue.

## Hypothesis Testing

### Smokers vs Non-Smokers Claim Amount

* **Test:** Independent T-Test
* **Result:** No statistically significant difference found.

### Claim Amount by Region

* **Test:** One-Way ANOVA
* **Result:** No statistically significant regional difference found.

### Claim Status vs Claim Type

* **Test:** Chi-Square Test
* **Result:** No significant association identified.

### Risk Score vs Claim Amount

* **Test:** Pearson Correlation
* **Result:** No significant relationship observed.
